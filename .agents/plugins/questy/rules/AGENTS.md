# Questy R&D Core Intelligence Rules

Whenever working on research, analysis, and architecture inside this repository:

1. **Evidentiary Rigor**: Every technical claim must be traceable to primary sources, verifiable documentation, or empirical benchmarks.
2. **Epistemic Honesty**: Clearly distinguish between established empirical facts, probabilistic models, and speculative hypotheses.
3. **Structured Delivery**: Utilize the modular `/ques-*` skills to separate inquiry, source critique, structural modeling, and executive synthesis.
4. **Zero-Bias Isolation**: Avoid premature convergence on preferred conclusions; actively evaluate counter-arguments and failure modes.
